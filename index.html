01Science

AMAZING FACTS

Why Is the Sky Blue?

Air molecules scatter shorter blue wavelengths of sunlight more strongly than longer red wavelengths. This effect is called Rayleigh scattering. The exact colour we see can change with the atmosphere and the position of the Sun.

02AI

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of computing that builds systems able to perform tasks such as recognising patterns, understanding language, generating content, and making predictions. AI tools can be helpful, but their answers should be checked for accuracy.

03Internet

TECHNOLOGY

How Does the Internet Work?

The internet is a global network of connected devices. When you open a website, your device sends requests through networks to a server. The server sends back files and data that your browser uses to display the page.

04Security

DIGITAL TIPS

How to Create a Strong Password

Use a long, unique password for every account. A password manager can help you store them safely. Turn on multi-factor authentication where available, and never share one-time verification codes with people who contact you unexpectedly.

05Cloud

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing lets people use services such as storage, databases, and computing power over the internet. It can make files easier to access across devices, but users should review privacy settings and keep backup copies of important data.

06Weather

AMAZING FACTS

Why Do We See Lightning Before Hearing Thunder?

Light travels much faster than sound through air. During a storm, the flash reaches your eyes before the sound wave reaches your ears. Counting the seconds between the flash and thunder can give a rough estimate of how far away the lightning is.

07Safety

DIGITAL TIPS

Five Ways to Stay Safer Online

Keep your operating system and apps updated, use unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, check website addresses carefully, and back up important files. Be cautious with unexpected attachments and urgent messages asking for money or private information.

08Software

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Open-Source Software?

Open-source software makes its source code available under a licence that allows people to inspect it and, depending on the licence, modify or redistribute it. Many developers collaborate on open-source projects, but users should still download software from trustworthy sources.

09QR Codes

TECHNOLOGY

What Does a QR Code Do?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as a website link or short text. Scan codes only from sources you trust, and check the destination URL before entering passwords or payment details.

10Energy

SCIENCE

How Do Solar Panels Make Electricity?

Solar panels use photovoltaic cells to convert light into electrical energy. Their output depends on sunlight, panel angle, shading, temperature, and system design. Batteries can store some of the generated energy for later use.

11Cybersecurity

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity means protecting devices, networks, accounts, and data from unauthorised access or damage. Good habits include installing updates, using multi-factor authentication, limiting app permissions, and learning how to recognise scams.

12Mobile

DIGITAL TIPS

How to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer

Reduce screen brightness when practical, use battery saver mode, review apps that consume unusually high power, and keep your phone away from extreme heat. Battery settings vary by model, so check your device manufacturer's guidance for specific advice.

13Web Development

TECHNOLOGY

What Is HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?

HTML structures the content of a webpage, CSS controls its appearance and layout, and JavaScript adds interaction. Together, these technologies are the foundation of many websites, from simple information pages to complex web applications.

14Nature

AMAZING FACTS

Why Does Ice Float on Water?

When water freezes, its molecules form a crystal structure that takes up more space than the same mass of liquid water. This makes ice less dense than liquid water, so it floats. This property also helps lakes freeze from the top down.

15Security

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication adds another verification step beyond a password. That second step may be an authenticator app, security key, or other supported method. It can make account theft harder even if a password is exposed.

16Scam Awareness

DIGITAL TIPS

How to Spot a Fake Website

Look closely at the domain name, be cautious of spelling mistakes and unrealistic offers, and avoid signing in through links in unexpected messages. A padlock or HTTPS connection encrypts traffic but does not, by itself, prove that a website is honest.

17Machine Learning

TECHNOLOGY

What Is Machine Learning?

Machine learning is a branch of AI in which systems learn patterns from examples or data rather than relying only on hand-written rules. Results depend on data quality, model design, and how the system is evaluated.

18GPS

SCIENCE

How Do GPS Devices Find Your Location?

GPS receivers estimate their location by measuring signals from satellites and calculating how long the signals took to arrive. A clear view of the sky often improves performance, while buildings and other obstacles can affect accuracy.

19Data

TECHNOLOGY

What Is a Data Backup and Why Is It Important?

A backup is an extra copy of important files that can be used if the originals are lost, damaged, or encrypted by malware. Keep backups separate from your main device and test occasionally that you can restore the files.

20Computers

DIGITAL TIPS

Simple Steps to Improve Computer Performance

Keep enough free storage space, remove apps you no longer use, install trusted updates, and check which programs start automatically. If a computer remains slow, check its memory and drive health before buying replacement parts.
